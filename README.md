## How to follow these

Type the commands rather than pasting them where you can. Typing is slower and is most of the reason the second part feels easier than the first.

When something does not work, check [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md) before changing three things at once. Almost every failure in this series has one of five causes, and they are all on that page.

<!-- widget:callout type=note -->

Each part ends with a check you can run yourself. If the check fails, stop there — part two on a broken part one produces errors that have nothing to do with what you are reading.
